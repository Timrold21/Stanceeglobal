What We Do

Our Services

At Stancee Educational Services and Travel, we offer comprehensive education, travel, career, and relocation solutions designed to help our clients achieve their international goals.

Study Abroad Services

Comprehensive guidance for international education

At Stancee Educational Services, we provide comprehensive guidance to students seeking quality education abroad. We guide you through the choosing, applying, and settling phases to ensure your academic journey starts with total confidence.

What Our Services Include:

  • University & College Selection Matching your academic profile with elite global institutions.
  • Course & Career Counselling Aligning your course selection with international career demands.
  • Admission Application Processing Managing direct submissions and tracking admissions cycle timelines.
  • Statement of Purpose & Motivation Guidance Personalized coaching to craft compelling essays and personal reviews.
  • CV & Academic Document Review Reviewing transcript documents, portfolios, and academic profiles.
  • Student Visa Guidance & Documentation Expert document compilation and visa interview coaching support.
  • Education Loan & Financial Guidance Advising on financial sponsor proofs and funding compliance.
  • Pre-departure Orientation Essential cultural, academic, and relocation briefings.
  • Accommodation Guidance Helping you secure housing, hostels, or student rentals abroad.
  • Airport Pickup & Settlement Support Ensuring smooth transit and welcoming setups where available.
  • Dependant Visa Guidance Guidance for processing family/spouse visas where applicable.

Work Relocation Services

Ethical recruitment support and professional migration

We assist qualified candidates seeking international employment opportunities through ethical recruitment support and relocation guidance. We partner with licensed agencies to provide transparent global placement channels.

What Our Services Include:

  • Career Assessment & Eligibility Evaluation Assessing your profile against visa regulations and market demands.
  • CV & Cover Letter Preparation Reformatting profiles to fit ATS and global recruitment standards.
  • International Job Search Support Coaching on global portals and targeting overseas openings.
  • Access to Verified Sponsorship Jobs Connecting you to roles that explicitly offer visa sponsorships.
  • Recruitment Assessment & Profiling Candidate profiling to ensure direct alignment with global employers.
  • Interview Preparation & Coaching Conducting mock interviews, panel reviews, and behavioral drills.
  • Work Visa Guidance & Documentation Complete guidance on sponsorships, work permit files, and paperwork.
  • Relocation Planning & Pre-departure Support Assisting with essential settlement check-sheets and travel.
  • Work Relocation Webinars & Sessions Regular interactive briefings on global relocation frameworks.
  • Immigration Updates & Market Information Real-time analysis on shortage occupation lists and labor regulations.
  • Ongoing Relocation Advisory Services Direct consulting support even after visa acquisition and arrival.
